Musico · Apple HIG

    Everything your local library wishes streaming apps kept

    Built to keep your folder hierarchy intact while letting you mount cloud playlists and write lightweight extensions when you want them.

    📁

    Folder-First & Safe Relocate

    Mount or drag-and-drop folders without flattening your tree. Relocate reconnects moved drives while keeping stats, and deletions let you choose Remove from Library or Delete on disk.

    ☁️

    Cloud Hangouts & Offline Sync

    Mount YouTube, Bilibili, and Google Drive (zero-login public links or optional OAuth 2.0) inside Folders, Save to Local Folder, and incrementally Update from Cloud.

    🎶

    Multiple Independent Queues

    Spin up parallel queues for work, commute, or midnight lo-fi — each keeping its active track, timestamp, shuffle, and repeat mode, with deduplicated Play Next and Outlook-style swipe actions + Undo.

    🎤

    Local-First & Cloud Lyrics

    Playback checks Imported, Sidecar, Same-Folder Fuzzy Match, and Embedded tags offline, with custom font size, alignment & Sync to current line, plus on-demand Search Online.

    🧩

    Sandboxed JS Extensions

    One CommonJS .musico.js or .musicoext runs in JavaScriptCore, QuickJS, and Jint with strict permissions.hosts allowlists, QR/Web Login capture, and cookies.txt import.

    🎨

    Custom Appearance, Mini-Player & Stats

    Style presets, custom backgrounds & WCAG 2.1 contrast check, Command Palette, Floating Mini-Player, sleep timers, Top Artists/Albums stats, and inline tag editing.
