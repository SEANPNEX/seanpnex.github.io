Influence sets the method: one line for associates, line by line for control.

Degree of influence → accounting treatment · darker = more control · one-line = the investee is one asset and one income line · line-by-line = every asset, liability, revenue and expense is added (x% or 100%)
One-line (equity method): the investor shows only Investment in on the B/S and its share of the investee's NI on the I/S. Line-by-line: proportionate consolidation adds the venturer's x% of each line; the acquisition method adds 100% of each line, with the outside owners' share shown as noncontrolling interest.
