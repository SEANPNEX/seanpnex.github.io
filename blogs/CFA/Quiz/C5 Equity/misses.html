Equity Quiz Misses ​

2026-11-15 — Quiz misses ​

  • EQ M4 · EV/EBITDA vs EV/FCFF — Gap: Identified A as more expensive but couldn't explain why; thought FCFF has the same blind spot because it adds back D&A.
  • EQ M4 · Justified trailing P/E — Gap: Used the retention ratio b=0.6 instead of the payout 1−b=0.4, and left out (1+g) for the trailing P/E.
  • EQ M6 · DLOC from control premium — Gap: Used the 25% control premium directly as the DLOC, and got 40%.
  • EQ M6 · Excess earnings method — Gap: Said intangibles are taken from financial reports and usually not included.
  • EQ M6 · Normalize earnings, any buyer — Gap: Said normalize for the strategic buyer only.

2026-11-16 — Quiz struggles ​

  • EQ M4 · Justified trailing P/S — Struggle (got it right): had to infer the inputs — retention from ΔRE/NI, g from ROE ×b, and PM0=E0/S0 from NI/sales; the trailing form includes (1+g).
  • EQ M3 · FCFE from sales growth, target DR — Struggle (got it right): didn't know how to infer investment from sales growth (rate ×ΔSales; net FCInv already nets out depreciation; equity bears only (1−DR)).

2026-11-16 — Quiz misses ​

  • EQ M6 · Normalized operating income, tax the add-back — Gap: Added the 480k pre-tax to after-tax operating income and got 1,355k.
  • EQ M5 · Multistage RI, P/B terminal — Gap: Used the terminal premium undiscounted and got 18.76.
  • EQ M3 · WCInv and FCInv from the balance sheet — Gap: Didn't know how to infer WCInv from the balance sheet, and used Δ(total assets − current assets) as FCInv.
  • EQ M3 · FCFE from statements, net borrowing — Gap: Picked FCFF (1,154) instead of FCFE.
  • EQ M3 · FCF vs DDM, control perspective — Gap: Said “DDM is superior to FCF if there is a change in control” was correct.
