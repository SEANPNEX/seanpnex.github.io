## 2026-10-13 — Steady state

**Q:** When does capital deepening stop? Relate \(sy\) to required investment.

**Covered:** Steady state is where \(sy = sAk^{\alpha}\) equals \(\bigl(\frac{\theta}{1-\alpha}+\delta+n\bigr)k\). Capital deepening stops at \(k^*\).

**Gap:** Pin the intersection of the savings curve and the required-investment ray, not a verbal “equilibrium” without the two sides.

## 2026-10-13 — Saving rate

**Q:** What does a higher saving rate do to steady-state \(k\) and \(y\), and to long-run growth of \(y\)?

**Covered:** \(\uparrow s\) raises \(k^*\) and \(y^*\) (a **level** effect). Long-run growth of \(y\) is **not** permanently higher. \(s\in[0,1]\) caps how far the lift can go.

**Gap:** Separate the level shift from a permanent growth-rate shift, and remember the \(s\le 1\) ceiling.

Source: Animus quiz handoff (after the quiz, not mid-quiz).


## 2026-10-13 — Convergence

**Q:** Country A has potential GDP growth well above \(G^*\); Country B is near \(G^*\). Where does each country’s growth come from?

**Covered:** A (\(G > G^*\)) is still converging via **capital deepening**. B near \(G^*\) gets growth mostly from **TFP** (and labor), not from capital investment.

**Gap:** Map “above steady-state growth” to ongoing capital deepening, and “at \(G^*\)” to TFP/labor.

Source: Animus quiz handoff (after the quiz, not mid-quiz).


## 2026-10-13 — Labor growth

**Q:** What happens to steady-state \(k\) and \(y\) when labor-force growth \(n\) rises?

**Covered:** \(\uparrow n\) steepens the required-investment line, so equilibrium \(k^*\) and \(y^*\) fall.

**Gap:** \(n\) enters the ray’s slope, not the savings curve — the intersection moves left and down.

Source: Animus quiz handoff (after the quiz, not mid-quiz).


## 2026-10-13 — TFP vs deepening

**Q:** What shifts the production curve, and what only moves along it?

**Covered:** Higher \(A\) (TFP) and \(\alpha\) shape or shift the curve. Capital deepening moves **along** a given curve.

**Gap:** Do not call a move along \(y=Ak^{\alpha}\) a TFP shift.

Source: Animus quiz handoff (after the quiz, not mid-quiz).
