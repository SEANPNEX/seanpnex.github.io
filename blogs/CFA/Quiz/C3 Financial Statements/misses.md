# FSA / Intercorporate Investments — quiz misses

## 2026-10-14 — Debt FVOCI impairment (ECL vs AOCI vs B/S)

**Q:** Debt FVOCI: FV falls 980→960, ECL $15. What hits I/S, what hits AOCI, and what carrying amount shows on B/S?

**My answer:** ECL $15 hits I/S; also thought $15 hits the asset on B/S; RE −15; AOCI −5 (or total FV move −20).

**Correct:**
- I/S / RE: **−15** (ECL)
- AOCI: **−5** (= −20 FV drop + 15 ECL already in earnings)
- B/S asset stays at **FV 960** — ECL does **not** write the asset down again under debt FVOCI (unlike AMC)

**Gap:** Confused AMC-style asset write-down with FVOCI’s FV carrying amount; AOCI split was otherwise close.

## 2026-10-15 — Equity method: upstream/downstream unrealized profit

**Q1:** Upstream sale (BY/SY): SY sells goods costing 600 to BY (20% investor) for 1,000; BY still holds them at year end. How much does BY eliminate, and when does it come back?

**My answer:** Eliminate the full 400; it comes back when the goods are sold to a third party.

**Correct:**
- Eliminate only BY's share: $20\%\times400=\mathbf{80}$ (equivalently, adjust SY's NI by $-400$, then take 20%)
- Released when the goods are sold to an outside party (pro rata) ✓

**Gap:** Eliminated the full unrealized profit instead of the investor's % share.

**Q2:** Upstream sale of an asset the buyer uses as a fixed asset (DJ/EJ: profit 300, 10-year straight line). How does the unrealized profit come back?

**My answer:** Through amortization.

**Correct:** Through **depreciation** of the fixed asset: $300/10=30$ a year gross, investor share $20\%\times30=\mathbf{6}$ a year.

**Gap:** Wording: a fixed asset is depreciated, not amortized (amortization is for intangibles).
