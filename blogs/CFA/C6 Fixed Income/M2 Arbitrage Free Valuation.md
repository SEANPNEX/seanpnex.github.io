# Arbitrage Free Valuation

Most of learned model in CFA is non-arbitrage free model.

Arbitrage are violations of one price law

Example 

| Asset | Price Today | Payoff in One Year Later |
| ----- | ----------- | ------------------------ |
| A     | $0.952381   | $1                       |
| B     | $95         | $105                     |
| C     | $100        | $105                     |
| D     | $200        | $220                     |



T = 0 short A long B

T = 1 sell B buy A to repay
