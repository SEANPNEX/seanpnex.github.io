## How I use these notes

1. **Plan:** choose the week’s outcome from the relevant learning plan.
2. **Learn:** read a module and restate its main idea without looking.
3. **Apply:** complete calculations, questions, code, or a short real-world task.
4. **Diagnose:** record why an incorrect answer or failed implementation seemed reasonable.
5. **Review:** revisit difficult material after two days, one week, and three weeks.

The goal is not to accumulate pages. A note is useful when it helps me retrieve a concept, make a decision, or solve a problem.

## Note conventions

- **Important** callouts identify distinctions that are easy to confuse.
- **Exam focus** sections prioritize testable CFA material.
- **For understanding** sections explain intuition beyond the required syllabus.
- Worked examples show the calculation structure before interpreting the result.
- Interactive charts and code examples are supporting tools; the written conclusion remains the primary takeaway.
